Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Data Collected

We collect the following personal information to provide our services:

  • Name and Phone Number
  • Hospital/Clinic Name and Delivery Address
  • Medical Registration details
  • Push Notification Tokens

2. Purpose of Collection

Your data is collected strictly for the purpose of order fulfillment, account verification, and communicating important updates about your orders.

3. Data Processors

We use industry-standard third-party processors to securely store and handle your data:

  • Supabase / AWS: For secure database hosting and authentication.
  • Cloudflare: For secure network routing and static hosting.

4. Right to Erasure (Account Deletion)

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. You can delete your account directly within the Speqtus Medical app (Profile > Delete My Account), or by visiting our Account Deletion page for instructions on how to request deletion via email.

5. Contact & Grievance Officer

For any privacy-related concerns or to reach our Grievance Officer, please email us at: privacy@speqtus.com.
