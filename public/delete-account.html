How to Delete Your Speqtus Medical Account

We provide two ways for you to permanently delete your account and personal data from our systems.

Option 1: Self-Serve In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Speqtus Medical app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm your choice in the alert dialog. Your account and personal details will be deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team.

Please send an email to support@speqtus.com from the email address or phone number associated with your account.

Note regarding order history: To comply with financial and tax regulations, your past order history (items and prices) will be retained. However, this data will be completely anonymized—your name, clinic name, and delivery address will be permanently removed from all past records.
